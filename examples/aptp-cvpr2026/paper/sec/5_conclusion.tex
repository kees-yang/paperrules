\section{Conclusion}
This work challenges the necessity of the Strictly Paired Training Paradigm (SPTP) in infrared and visible image fusion by systematically exploring UnPaired and Arbitrarily Paired Training Paradigms (UPTP and APTP). Theoretically, UPTP and SPTP are shown to be complementary subsets of the more flexible APTP. The proposed solution significantly expands the volume and diversity of cross-modal relationships from a limited dataset. Through three lightweight baselines and novel loss functions, experiments demonstrate that UPTP and APTP can train models on severely limited, content-inconsistent data, achieving performance comparable to models trained on a dataset 100 times larger under SPTP. This finding substantially reduces data collection costs and enhances model robustness from a data perspective.
